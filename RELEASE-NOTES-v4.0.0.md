What's new:
----------------------------------------------------
- Auto update: X360GameHack tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you. The update notice is signed and every download is checked before it runs, so nobody can slip in a fake update. The title bar shows whether you are on the latest version.
- Paid mods: the mods of X360DeRankTool - 12 games - are ported into X360GameHack. A licence unlocks them and is tied to your PC; the mod files only come from the licence server, sealed, and only for a licensed PC.
- Drive Explorer: open Xbox 360 and original Xbox hard drives on your PC (SATA, USB or a drive image) - every partition, read and write, drag files in and out, saves, recover deleted files, back up, Safely remove - with an undo journal for every change to a real drive. Tested on 2 live drives: one formatted in an Xbox 360 used on an RGH, and a 1 TB drive formatted with FATXplorer from a TSOP'd original Xbox.
- Saves: every save listed with its game and profile; rehash & resign in one click, with the right IDs picked for you from your profile, your drive or Xenia; create a save package from your own files; the GPD viewer; and saves put straight into Xenia.
- Profiles: see any profile - gamertag, games, gamerscore and achievements; change the gamertag; add games by Title ID with their real achievements. (Unlocking achievements and avatar awards, the gamer picture and gamercard editors are in BETA.)
- Title Updates: finds the title updates for your game and installs them in the right folder for your console, USB stick or Xenia - Xenia applies them (tested with real games).
- X360Packages: open, verify and extract any package (CON, LIVE, PIRS - saves, DLC, title updates, arcade games, themes); replace, add and delete files inside it, change its names and pictures, and rehash it in one click.
- Xenia: games list, one-click Play, every Xenia setting, and saves / title updates / DLC installed the way Xenia keeps them.
- XEX Disassembler: a full Xbox 360 disassembler with a decompiler that shows the C, and export to Ghidra.
- Built-in engines for XEX, STFS packages, ISO / XISO and Games on Demand - checked byte for byte against the trusted tools.
- Original Xbox: XBE tools, and Play in xemu from your drive.
- A brand-new look, themes, Q & A, and a sidebar with everything in its place.

About the BETA and BRICKS POSSIBLE labels:
----------------------------------------------------
- Anything with a BETA label has not been tested end to end yet. It could have problems, or it could work perfectly fine. If you are not an advanced user, please hold off on BETA features until the next big release.
- BRICKS POSSIBLE (Flash Dumps): writing a NAND can brick a console. I have plenty of consoles to test on, but with everything else going on I haven't had the time to test the flash tabs yet. Everything there works on a copy of your dump.
- What is tested is listed in TESTING.md (included).
